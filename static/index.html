Why does FP-Growth scale better?

Apriori relies on the Apriori property (downward closure) and performs iterative candidate generation ($L_{k-1} \bowtie L_{k-1}$ join) followed by repeated complete passes over the database to count support. For dense datasets or lower support, candidate generation produces a combinatorial explosion of $O(2^{|I|})$ itemsets. In contrast, FP-Growth constructs a compact Frequent Pattern Tree (FP-Tree) in exactly two scans, then recursively mines conditional pattern bases using divide-and-conquer without generating candidate itemsets.

Parameter Justification

Analyzing the grid reveals that at support < 1%, rule counts explode into hundreds of weak associations. At support ≥ 3% and confidence ≥ 35%, rule count stabilizes into statistically solid, actionable grocery bundles with high lift.

Selected: Min Support = 3.0%, Min Confidence = 35%
